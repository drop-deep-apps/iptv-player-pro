1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how IPTV Player Pro handles information when you use our Android application. We designed the application with a strict local-first architecture so your media sources and viewing activity remain under your control.

1.1 Information We Do Not Collect

  • No Developer Account Required: IPTV Player Pro does not ask you to create an account with us.
  • No Analytics or Advertising Trackers: The app does not include third-party ad networks, behavioral tracking SDKs, or telemetry services.
  • No Server-Side Storage: We do not operate servers that receive, log, inspect, or store your playlists, Xtream credentials, or viewing history.

1.2 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide core player functionality, the app stores the following information exclusively on your device's internal storage:

  • Playlists & Connection Details: M3U URLs, local M3U files you select, playlist names, and Xtream Codes portal credentials (portal URL, username, password) that you explicitly enter.
  • Library & Preferences: Channel/VOD metadata parsed from your playlists, favorite items, playback resume positions (for VOD and episodes), and app settings (such as license confirmation and delete-confirmation preferences).

1.3 Third-Party Network Connections

When you import a playlist, load artwork, or play a stream, your device connects directly to the third-party server URLs included in the playlist or stream you provided. Those external servers are operated by independent third parties and are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

1.4 Children's Privacy

IPTV Player Pro is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

2. Data Security, Permissions & Deletion

2.1 App Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely to download playlists you provide, retrieve channel/VOD artwork referenced in your playlists, and stream media directly to your device.

2.2 Backup & Credential Protection

To protect your privacy and prevent sensitive connection details from leaving your device, the local SQLite database containing playlists and Xtream credentials is explicitly excluded from Android cloud backups and automated device-to-device data transfers.

2.3 Your Data Control & Deletion Rights

  • You can delete individual playlists or remove favorite items inside the app at any time.
  • You can clear your saved VOD/episode resume positions via Settings → Clear Watch History.
  • Uninstalling IPTV Player Pro permanently removes all locally stored playlists, credentials, preferences, and playback history from your device.
